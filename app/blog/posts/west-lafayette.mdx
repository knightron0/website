It is really hard to put into words how awesome my experience at Purdue was, and I've been racking my brain trying to come up with a way to write this post. 
College is full of high-entropy moments, but it was defined just as much, for me, by the traditions that made it so hard to say goodbye. Here are a few!

--- 

It's 9 AM; I sprint to the shower. On the way, I bang on Leon's door loudly to wake him up. Ram freshens up as I get back. We run down the stairs and across the street to Earhart to catch the bus.

We try our best to convince Tejas and Sahir to stay, but they end up leaving. We're all in Joey's room, watching some horror movie. Then another, then another. 

Atharv, Tejas and I are the only ones awake. It's a Sunday morning, and the floor is mostly silent. We gradually wake everyone up. It's brunch time! We walk to Hillenbrand. Everyone gets the pancakes, etc. One of us gets the eggs (which are somehow green). Fun times!
